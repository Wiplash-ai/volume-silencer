Support

Let us turn down the problem.

VolumeSilencer works on normal webpages with HTML audio or video. Browser-owned pages and extension-store pages do not allow extensions to run.

The widget does not appear

Open a normal website, select the VolumeSilencer toolbar icon, and confirm the extension has access to that site. Refresh pages that were already open when the extension was installed.

Page volume and tab mute differ

Page volume controls HTML media elements. Tab mute silences all audio Chromium or Firefox attributes to the tab, including sources that are not ordinary media elements.

Report a problem

Open an issue in the public repository or email support@wiplash.ai. Include your browser version and the website where the behavior occurred, but do not include private page content.
